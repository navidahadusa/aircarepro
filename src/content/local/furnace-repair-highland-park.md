<h2>Furnace repair for Highland Park homes</h2>
<p>Highland Park homes often have variable-speed or two-stage furnaces, communicating thermostats and zoning. These advanced systems give excellent comfort but require technicians who understand their controls and fault codes. We diagnose electronically commutated blower motors, modulating gas valves and communication errors, as well as the basics like igniters and drains. Homes on wooded lots can also have vent terminals blocked by leaves or snow drifts, which we check on every call.</p>

<h2>About homes in Highland Park</h2>
<p>Highland Park stretches along the North Shore with a mix of historic homes in the Ravinia district, mid-century houses and large modern homes on wooded lots. Many homes here have multiple furnaces and air conditioners, zoned systems, high-efficiency boilers or radiant heat, and whole-home humidity control.</p><h3>Complex multi-system homes</h3><p>Homes with several furnaces, ACs or boilers need coordinated service and controls.</p><h3>Radiant and hydronic heat</h3><p>We service boilers that feed radiators and radiant floors, including zone controls and circulators.</p>

<h2>Furnace Repair problems we fix in Highland Park</h2>
<ul><li><strong>Igniter and flame sensor failures</strong></li><li><strong>Frozen or clogged condensate drains</strong></li><li><strong>Inducer motor and pressure switch faults</strong></li><li><strong>Blower motor and capacitor problems</strong></li><li><strong>Control board and limit switch issues</strong></li></ul><p>Most no-heat calls come down to a handful of causes: a failed igniter, a dirty flame sensor, a blocked condensate drain on a high-efficiency furnace, a pressure switch fault, a worn inducer motor or a control board issue. Our technicians read the furnace&#x27;s fault codes, test each component in the sequence of operation and check gas pressure and temperature rise, so we replace the part that actually failed instead of guessing. Before we leave, we run the furnace through full cycles and check for safe venting and carbon monoxide.</p>

<h2>Signs you need furnace repair</h2>
<ul><li>Furnace runs but blows cold air</li><li>Furnace won&#x27;t start or keeps shutting off (short cycling)</li><li>Banging, squealing or rattling noises</li><li>Blinking error light on the control board</li><li>Some rooms are much colder than others</li><li>Higher gas bills with no change in use</li></ul>
