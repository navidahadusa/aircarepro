# GBP rename: evidence kit (Hannah Carter)

The owner submitted the Google Business Profile name change to **CHICAGO AIRCARE PRO** on 2026-11-20. Google often reviews name changes. It may approve silently, show "Your edits are under review", or ask for proof (documents upload or a short **video verification**). This page is what to have ready.

## Don't do while it's under review
- Don't change the name again, or edit the category, phone, service area or hours. Several edits at once often trigger re-verification or a suspension check.
- Don't add keywords to the name ("CHICAGO AIRCARE PRO HVAC Repair Chicago" breaks Google's rules).
- Don't create a new profile under the new name. Keep the existing one (its reviews and history stay).
