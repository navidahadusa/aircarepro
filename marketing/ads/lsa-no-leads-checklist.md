# Local Services Ads: "live but no leads" checklist (Lauren Hayes) · 2026-11-23

**Owner's report (2026-11-23):** LSA is now connected to the **new** account and shows as open/active, but **no leads yet**.
LSA only shows ads when **every** item below is in order, and a new or re-verified profile often needs a few days before leads start. Go through this list in the LSA dashboard (ads.google.com/localservices) and send Lauren a screenshot of each page marked 📸.

## 1. Is it really serving? 📸 (Dashboard / Overview)
- [ ] Status says **Active** (not "Pending", "Verification in progress", "Paused", "Budget limited" or "Insurance/license needed").
- [ ] No red banner about documents, background checks or the **old name** or **old website** (see `lsa-rename-guide.md`).
- [ ] The business name shows **CHICAGO AIRCARE PRO** and the website **https://aircareprohvac.com/**.

## 2. Budget and bidding 📸 (Budget)
- [ ] A **weekly budget** is set. LSA charges per lead, so money is only spent when a real lead comes in.
- [ ] Bidding: for a new profile choose **Maximize leads**. If it's on "Max per lead" with a low number, LSA can win almost nothing in Chicago.
- [ ] Suggested weekly budget from the split: **about $140/week** ($600/month, see `ads-plan.md` → Budgets). Change it only when you decide; Lauren doesn't change budgets.

## 3. Hours 📸 (Profile → Business hours)
- [ ] **Open 24 hours, 7 days.** LSA only shows the ad during the hours listed here. If the hours are short, the ad sleeps at night, exactly when "no heat" calls come in.
- [ ] Any "pause when unavailable / away" setting is **off**.

## 4. Job types and service areas 📸 (Profile → Services / Service area)
- [ ] **Every job type you do is on:** furnace repair/installation/maintenance, boiler repair/installation, heat pump, AC repair/installation/maintenance, mini-split, ductwork, thermostat, air quality, plus any "emergency" options offered.
- [ ] **Service area = all our areas:** Chicago North, Northwest and West Side ZIPs, plus Evanston, Skokie, Wilmette, Highland Park, Des Plaines, Arlington Heights, Schaumburg, Naperville (the same list as `marketing/gbp/gbp-website-match.md`). A tiny area means few searches.

## 5. Reviews and Google profile link 📸 (Reviews)
- [ ] The **renamed Google Business Profile is linked**, so its reviews show on the ad. A new LSA account with **0 reviews** ranks low. Reviews are one of the biggest LSA ranking factors.
- [ ] Keep asking every customer for a Google review (`/review/` link and QR). (Hannah Carter, `review-engine` skill)

## 6. Lead routing and responsiveness 📸 (Settings → Notifications / Leads)
- [ ] Leads go to **(773) 644-8227** (LSA uses its own tracking number that forwards to your phone). Make a test: does your phone ring?
- [ ] **Message leads** and **booking requests** are on, and notifications go to the email and phone you actually check.
- [ ] Look in the **Leads tab, including "Archived"**, in case leads came in and were missed.
- [ ] **Answer every LSA call** and reply to messages fast. Google lowers ranking for missed calls and slow replies.

## 7. Profile quality
- [ ] Profile photo/logo (new logo), a short bio in plain words, license #3065598 displayed.
- [ ] Only real facts (years in business, etc.). Nothing the team or the owner can't prove.

## 8. Give it time, then measure
- After everything above is green, give it **5–7 days**. Winter is high season for heating searches, so if it's still silent after a week with 24/7 hours, Maximize leads, all job types and areas, the cause is almost always **reviews** (too few) or **verification** (not fully passed). Then contact LSA support from the dashboard.
- Each week, send Lauren: leads, valid vs invalid, cost per lead. She disputes invalid leads (wrong service, outside the area, spam) within Google's allowed window.
