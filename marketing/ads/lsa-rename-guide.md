# Google Guaranteed / Local Services Ads (LSA): rebrand checklist (Rachel Morgan + Hannah Carter), 2026-11-21

LSA verifies the **business**, so a name change usually needs the profile updated **and** new documents in the new name. Google checks that the name on the LSA profile, the license and the insurance all match exactly. (Labels in the LSA dashboard change from time to time; the steps below describe the usual path.)

## 1. Update the LSA profile
ads.google.com/localservices → sign in → **Profile** (or "Profile & budget") → Business details:
- Business name: **CHICAGO AIRCARE PRO**
- Website: **https://aircareprohvac.com/** (replace any old website address)
- Phone (773) 644-8227 · service areas and job types matching the site (`marketing/gbp/gbp-website-match.md`)
- **Link the renamed Google Business Profile** (LSA shows the reviews from the linked GBP).

## 2. Upload documents in the NEW name (Verification section)
- City of Chicago business license **#3065598** showing CHICAGO AIRCARE PRO (if it still shows the old name, update it with the City first).
- Illinois Secretary of State filing for the name change (Articles of Amendment or Assumed Name certificate).
- **Insurance certificate (general liability)** listing CHICAGO AIRCARE PRO as the insured. Ask the insurance agent for an updated COI; it's usually quick.
- The owner/technician background checks are tied to people, and usually don't need to be redone for a name change. Google will say if they do.

## 3. If LSA support says the "old website / old name" is still showing
- Make sure no profile field (LSA, GBP, Google Ads account) still lists the old website.
- **Old domain (shadheatingandcooling.com):** if it still shows a site with the old name, set it up at its registrar (GoDaddy, Namecheap, Google Domains/Squarespace…) as **domain forwarding → https://aircareprohvac.com, permanent (301)**. Don't leave a live site with the old name: Google treats it as a second, conflicting business. Keep the domain registered (and forwarding) for at least a year so old links and listings land on the new site.
- Ask LSA support (Help in the dashboard → contact) to **update the business name due to a legal rebrand**, and attach the documents from step 2.

## 4. While LSA is under review
- Don't change the GBP name or category again. Keep weekly GBP posts and photos going (activity helps).
- Keep collecting Google reviews (`/review/` link and QR). LSA ranking uses reviews.

## Budget rule
The team doesn't change LSA budget, bidding or pause/resume anything. Owner only.
