## Business card (3.5 × 2 in, 0.125 in bleed, 300 dpi)
`business-card-front.pdf|png`, `business-card-back.pdf|png`. Front: logo, name, Heating & Cooling, 24/7. Back: phone (calls and texts), website, email, services, credentials, license, QR "Scan to book".
Order: Vistaprint / MOO / a local Chicago print shop → "upload your own design", 16 pt matte or soft-touch, 500 cards. Optional: add the owner's name and title on the back (tell Chloe).

## Furnace service sticker (6 × 4 in, 0.0625 in bleed)
`furnace-sticker-6x4.pdf|png`. Write-in fields: service date, technician, next service due, filter size. Big 24/7 phone strip, QR "Scan to book service", gas/CO safety line.
Order as **white matte vinyl, permanent adhesive, heat-resistant** (rated for appliance surfaces). Techs fill it with a permanent marker after every visit and stick it on the furnace or boiler cabinet (not on hot flue pipes or labels/rating plates).
