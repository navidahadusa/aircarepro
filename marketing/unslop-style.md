# Unslop: write and design like a professional human agency (owner request, 2026-11-21)

The owner asked that everything the team ships reads and looks like the work of an experienced human agency, not an AI template. These rules apply to every page, post, GBP post and design.

## Writing (Maya Lin; everyone)
- **No em dashes (—) in visible copy.** Use a period, comma, colon or parentheses.
- **No filler or brochure words:** seamless, peace of mind, look no further, we've got you covered, hassle-free, rest assured, top-notch, cutting-edge, state-of-the-art, elevate, unlock, journey, navigate, "in today's…", "when it comes to", "plays a vital role".
- **Don't lean on trust adjectives** (honest, reliable, quality, expert, trusted). Show it with a fact instead: "you see the price before any work", "City of Chicago License #3065598", "we test gas pressure before replacing a part".
- **Specific beats general:** name the housing type (two-flat, greystone, bungalow), the part (circulator, low-water cutoff, flame sensor), the season, the neighborhood.
- **Vary rhythm:** short sentences next to longer ones; not every list has three items; not every paragraph ends with a CTA.
- **One idea per sentence, plain words.** Read it out loud: if a Chicago homeowner wouldn't say it, rewrite it.
- **Never invent** numbers, reviews, stories, prices or response times (unchanged rule).

## Design (Chloe Vance)
- **No label over every heading.** Use a small sentence-case kicker only where it adds information (e.g. "Free homeowner resource").
- **No icon-in-a-colored-box on every list item.** Use numbers, rules (thin dividers) and typography; keep icons for buttons and the few places where they help scanning.
- **Not every section is "heading + paragraph + grid of shadowed cards".** Alternate: photo + text, plain lists, full-width photo rows, short text-only sections.
- **Real photos large, decoration small.** No decorative glows or gradient blobs; light shadows (`--shadow`), 10px radius.
- **Fewer badges.** One license ribbon in the hero; credentials go in a plain list.
- Keep: brand colors, Plus Jakarta Sans + Inter, 44px tap targets, contrast AA, Lighthouse ≥ 95.
