# Outreach templates

Personalize every send: reference something specific on their site, lead with value to **their** audience, make one clear ask, keep it under 120 words. Placeholders are `{{like_this}}`.
Email templates must include a real sender name, the business's physical address and an opt-out line (CAN-SPAM).

---

## 1. Local sponsorship
**Subject:** {{business_name}} would love to support {{organization}}

Hi {{first_name}},

I'm {{sender_name}} with {{business_name}}, a local heating & cooling company here in {{city}}. We've followed {{organization}}'s {{specific_program}} and would love to support it this season.

Do you have sponsorship options for local businesses? We're happy to contribute {{offer: funds / equipment / volunteer hours}}.

Thanks,
{{sender_name}} · {{phone}} · {{address}}

---
