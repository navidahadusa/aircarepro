## How local HVAC companies actually win on Google
For "furnace repair near me" and similar searches, the map results are driven mainly by three things:
1. The Google profile and its reviews.
2. Consistent listings (citations) across the web.
3. The website's relevance.

Backlinks feed the third point and the website's authority overall. For a local company, **a few real local links beat many random ones**: chambers, neighborhood groups, local press, suppliers and partners. **We never buy links or use link networks.** Google penalizes that, and the site would lose what it has built.

Target: **3–5 relevant local referring domains per month**, plus 15–20 consistent listings in the first month.

## Ongoing presence (weekly)
- **Google profile:** one post per week (drafts ready) and photos every week or two.
- **Reviews:** ask every happy customer the same day (`/review/` link).
- **Facebook / Instagram:** reuse the weekly Google post. No team login needed; the owner posts.
- **Content:** a daily post or refresh on the website, so linkers have something to link to.

## What the team can't do (and won't pretend to)
- Log into the owner's accounts, receive verification codes or send email from info@. The team writes everything ready to paste; the owner sends or submits.
- Promise numbers. We report referring domains and listings from Search Console and the tracker, monthly.
