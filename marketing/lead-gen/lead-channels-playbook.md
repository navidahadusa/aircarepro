| # | Action | Who |

| 1.3 | Add **real job photos** to the profile every week or two (before/after, the van, equipment you installed). No customer faces without permission. | You |
| 1.4 | Reply to every review (team drafts replies on request). | You / Hannah |
| 1.5 | Send us GBP **Performance** and **Reviews** screenshots weekly; Hannah logs calls, direction requests and website clicks. | You |
