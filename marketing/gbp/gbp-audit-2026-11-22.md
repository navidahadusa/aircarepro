## Findings (most important first)
1. **The new name has almost no footprint outside Google.** A public web search for "CHICAGO AIRCARE PRO" (2026-11-22) returns no directory listing under the new name. The old "Shad Heating and Cooling" doesn't show up either. Google trusts a business more when Yelp, BBB, Bing, Apple, Facebook and directories repeat the same name, phone and website. Right now only GBP and the website do. **This is the main ranking weakness.**
2. **Opening date shows January 1, 2026** (owner screenshot). That tells Google and customers the business is under a year old. If the company operated earlier under the old name, set the real start date.
3. **Description had prices** ($69.99 diagnostic, $16.99/month plan). Replaced with a price-free version (`gbp-website-match.md` §2), 2026-11-22.
4. **Recent rename (2026-11-20).** Google re-checks a profile after a name change. A temporary drop in visibility and in the profile-strength score (owner saw 91 → 80) is common for a few weeks. Don't change the name or category again while it settles.
5. **Few reviews.** The site shows 7 verbatim Google reviews (owner screenshots). Map Pack competitors in Chicago usually have far more. Reviews that mention the service and neighborhood are the fastest Map Pack lever (`review-engine` skill).
6. **Website clicks from GBP aren't separated in GA4.** In the last 28 days: 108 sessions, 89 Direct, 12 Organic Search. The GBP website field should use the UTM link (`gbp-website-match.md` §1) so profile clicks show up as `google / organic / gbp`.
7. **Website side is ready.** Search Console 28 days to 2026-11-19: 1,647 impressions (up from 1,095), average position 49.4, 0 clicks, still early. GA4: 4 clicks to Housecall Pro booking.
