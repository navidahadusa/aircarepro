## Owner request: PageSpeed showed "LCP Error! NO_LCP" on the homepage (Chloe Vance, Sophia Reynolds)
- **What it meant:** Lighthouse couldn't find a Largest Contentful Paint at all, so the "100" Performance score left out its most important metric and wasn't real. Reproduced locally: NO_LCP in 4/4 Lighthouse runs on the live homepage.
- **How we found it:** bisected the homepage section by section. Hero only → LCP OK; the break came with section 9. Inside it, removing the reviews carousel's `scroll-snap-type` alone fixed it (fonts, scripts and text clamping ruled out). A snap container makes a tiny automatic scroll while the page loads; Chrome treats it as a scroll and stops measuring LCP before recording anything.
- **Fix 1 (`ReviewsCarousel.astro`):** snapping now switches on at the first touch, wheel, focus or key press on the carousel, or when it auto-advances, never during page load. The carousel behaves the same for visitors.
- **Fix 2 (`index.astro`):** the hero photo is portrait (1050×1400) but shows as a wide strip on phones. Phones and tablets now get a pre-cropped 16:10 version (480/768/1050 px, 19–101 KB instead of 160 KB). Desktop unchanged.
- **Result (Lighthouse mobile, local build):** homepage LCP **2.6 s** (was NO_LCP), Performance **95**, Accessibility 100, Best Practices 100, SEO 100. /reviews/ 99 · /about/ 97. 0 horizontal overflow (iPhone, 1366px). check 0 errors · build ✓ · 0 orphans.
- **Tried and dropped:** preloading the fonts. It made LCP disappear more often, so it isn't in the fix.
- **Owner:** merge the PR, then re-run PageSpeed on the homepage.
