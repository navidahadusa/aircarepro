# Review playbook: CHICAGO AIRCARE PRO
Owners: **Hannah Carter** (profiles), **Rachel Morgan** (requests and tracking) · Approved by **Sophia Reynolds**

**Rules:** only real customers, after real jobs. Never pay, discount, or offer gifts for reviews. Never ask only happy customers ("review gating"). Never write reviews for customers or post them from company devices. **Yelp forbids asking for reviews**: never ask for Yelp reviews; just let Yelp exist.

## The routine after every job (2 minutes)
1. **At the end of the job, in person:** "If we did a good job today, a Google review really helps a small Chicago company like ours. I'll text you the link."
2. **Within 2 hours, by text** (automatic in Housecall Pro → Settings → Review requests, or send manually).
3. **3 days later:** one reminder if there's no review. Never more than one.
4. **Reply to every review within 48 hours** (templates below).

## Text message (SMS)
> Hi {first name}, this is {tech name} from Chicago Aircare Pro. Thanks for having us today! If you were happy with the {service}, would you leave us a quick Google review? It takes 30 seconds: {GOOGLE_REVIEW_LINK} Thank you!

## Reminder (day 3, once)
> Hi {first name}, quick reminder from Chicago Aircare Pro. If you have 30 seconds, your Google review helps neighbors find an honest HVAC company: {GOOGLE_REVIEW_LINK} Thanks again!

## Email
**Subject:** How did we do, {first name}?
> Thanks for choosing Chicago Aircare Pro for your {service}. If you were happy with the work, a short Google review helps other Chicago homeowners find us: {GOOGLE_REVIEW_LINK}
> If anything wasn't right, reply to this email or call (773) 644-8227 and we'll make it right.

## What helps reviews rank (ask naturally, never script the customer)
Customers who mention the **service** and the **neighborhood** ("fixed our boiler in Rogers Park") help local rankings. It's fine to say: "If you mention what we fixed, that helps other people with the same problem."

## Replying to reviews
- **5★:** "Thank you, {name}! Glad we could get your {furnace/boiler/AC} running again in {area}. We're here 24/7 if you ever need us. – Navid, Chicago Aircare Pro"
- **4★:** "Thanks for the review, {name}! If there's anything we could have done better, call us at (773) 644-8227. We'd love to hear it."
- **1–3★:** "We're sorry, {name}. That's not the experience we want for anyone. Please call Navid directly at (773) 644-8227 so we can make it right." (No arguing, no customer details in public.)

## Leave-behind card (print)
"Thanks for choosing Chicago Aircare Pro! Scan to leave a Google review." + QR code to `{GOOGLE_REVIEW_LINK}` + phone (773) 644-8227.

## Needed from the owner
- **Google review link:** Google Business Profile → **Ask for reviews** (or "Get more reviews") → copy the link (`https://g.page/r/…/review`). Send it to Sophia; the team adds it to the website (a `/review/` page and the reviews block), the QR card and these templates.
- Turn on **review requests in Housecall Pro**, after its public name shows CHICAGO AIRCARE PRO.
