**Rules:** only real customers, after real jobs. Never pay, discount, or offer gifts for reviews. Never ask only happy customers ("review gating"). Never write reviews for customers or post them from company devices. **Yelp forbids asking for reviews**: never ask for Yelp reviews; just let Yelp exist.

## The routine after every job (2 minutes)
1. **At the end of the job, in person:** "An honest Google review really helps a small Chicago company like ours. I'll text you the link." (Ask every customer, not only the happy ones.)
2. **Within 2 hours, by text** (automatic in Housecall Pro → Settings → Review requests, or send manually).
3. **3 days later:** one reminder if there's no review. Never more than one.
4. **Reply to every review within 48 hours** (templates below).

## What helps reviews rank (ask naturally, never script the customer)
Customers who mention the **service** and the **neighborhood** ("fixed our boiler in Rogers Park") help local rankings. It's fine to say: "If you mention what we fixed, that helps other people with the same problem."

## Replying to reviews
- **5★:** "Thank you, {name}! Glad we could get your {furnace/boiler/AC} running again in {area}. We're here 24/7 if you ever need us. – The Chicago Aircare Pro team"
- **4★:** "Thanks for the review, {name}! If there's anything we could have done better, call us at (773) 644-8227. We'd love to hear it."
- **1–3★:** "We're sorry, {name}. That's not the experience we want for anyone. Please call us directly at (773) 644-8227 so we can make it right." (No arguing, no customer details in public.)
